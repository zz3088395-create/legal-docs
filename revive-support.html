Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All restoration runs entirely on your device using built-in AI models. Your photos never leave your phone, and the App works fully offline.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open the upgrade screen in the App and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Revive, then tap Cancel. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does a photo fail to restore?

Very large images or unusual formats can occasionally fail. Try again with a JPEG or HEIC photo from your library. If the problem persists, email us the details (without the photo if it's private) and we'll investigate.
