Privacy Policy

SnapID — ID Photo Maker · Effective date: August 4, 2026

The short version

SnapID does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Period.

Your photos

All photo processing — including AI background removal — happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, never leave your phone, and are never accessible to us or to anyone else. SnapID works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Data collection

SnapID contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services. We collect nothing: no personal information, no usage data, no device identifiers.

Photo library access

SnapID asks for photo library permission only to let you pick a photo and to save the finished ID photo back to your library. This access is handled by iOS and used for nothing else.

Purchases

SnapID is a paid app sold through the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple. We never see your payment information.

Children's privacy

Since SnapID collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
