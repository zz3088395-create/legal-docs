Privacy Policy

Revive: AI Photo Restorer
Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Revive: AI Photo Restorer ("the App", "we") handles your information. The short version: your photos never leave your device, and we do not collect your personal data.

Photos Are Processed On-Device

All photo restoration and enhancement is performed entirely on your device using built-in AI models. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, never transmitted over the internet, and never accessed by us or any third party. Restored photos are saved only to your device's photo library, with your permission.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or browsing data. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases

The App offers optional paid upgrades (a subscription and a lifetime purchase). Payments are processed by Apple through your App Store account; we never see your payment details. To validate purchases and restore them across devices, the App uses RevenueCat, which processes a randomly generated anonymous identifier and your purchase history on our behalf. This identifier cannot be used to personally identify you. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library solely so you can select photos to restore and save the results. This access is used for no other purpose.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
